The Studio

Order is overrated.

Our work begins where polite ideas end. We build products with personality, mechanics with consequences, and worlds that feel worn-in before the first player or reader arrives.

A workshop, not a factory.

Bad Wizard Studio is an independent creative studio dedicated to original tabletop games, comics, digital media, fiction, illustration, and connected story worlds. Each project begins with a strong premise, a distinctive voice, and a question we cannot leave alone.

We sketch, test, cut, rewrite, rebuild, argue, laugh, and repeat until the final work feels deliberate—even when it looks gloriously unruly.

The Method

How a bad idea becomes a good artifact.

The workshop may look chaotic, but the craft is deliberate. Every project moves through a repeatable cycle of discovery, construction, testing, and refinement.

◈

Find the dangerous premise

We begin with a sharp question, contradiction, character, or social tension strong enough to carry an entire world.

⌘

Build the world and system

Lore, mechanics, visual language, and audience experience are developed together rather than bolted on afterward.

⚒

Prototype without mercy

We test quickly, break assumptions, remove weak ideas, and rebuild anything that does not produce the intended experience.

✦

Polish the artifact

Writing, design, illustration, usability, production, and presentation are refined until the object feels complete and unmistakably ours.
