How We Create

The tools may be digital, but the process is still hand-built: idea first, system second, polish last.

1. Find the hook

Every project begins with the part people will remember: the premise, mood, mechanic, joke, fear, or visual identity.

2. Build the experience

We define the audience, format, interaction, visual language, sound, platform, and practical production needs.

3. Prototype fast

Rough cuts, mockups, playable builds, sample audio, storyboards, and proof-of-concept artifacts expose weak ideas early.

4. Finish with teeth

Editing, polish, sound, motion, pacing, interface, and presentation are refined until the work feels deliberate and unmistakably Bad Wizard.

Cross-Media Studio

One world, many formats.

Bad Wizard Media exists to help ideas move between forms. A board game can become a trailer. A comic can become an animated teaser. A fictional world can become a podcast, interactive website, game prototype, or digital campaign.

The objective is not to force every idea into every medium. It is to find the medium that makes the idea hit hardest.
