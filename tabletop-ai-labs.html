Recommended loop

From draft to sharper table test.

AI Labs works best as an early intelligence layer before the real human work begins.

01 · Draft

Prepare rules

Write enough structure for goals, turns, actions, conflicts, scoring, and end states to be understood.

02 · Simulate

Probe the system

Run agent tests to identify risky questions and revision targets before gathering a human group.

03 · Position

Map the market

Compare the concept against similar games and current marketplace evidence without treating estimates as prophecy.

04 · Validate

Return to people

Prototype physically, test accessibility, observe real players, revise, and repeat until the game earns its table time.

Open the lab
