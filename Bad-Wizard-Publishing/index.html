About Bad Wizard Publishing

Bad Wizard Publishing is the illustrated-story arm of the Bad Wizard creative universe: independent, irreverent, genre-loving, and unwilling to sand down every sharp edge.

  • Original horror, satire, crime, pulp, and dark comedy.
  • Creator-forward development with a strong house identity.
  • Accessible digital previews and collectible physical editions.
  • Stories that can expand into games, animation, audio, and other formats.

Frequently Asked

Are these comics connected to Bad Wizard Games?

They share the same creative studio and may eventually cross into game settings, characters, campaigns, or adaptations, but each comic is designed to stand on its own.

Will the comics be digital or printed?

The planned model supports digital previews and physical editions, including potential limited or campaign-exclusive versions.

Can creators submit projects?

Yes. Use the contact form and include a concise hook, format, genre, synopsis, sample work, and your preferred collaboration structure.
